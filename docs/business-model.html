Why this model fits the buyer promise

Passport creation and preserving the buyer's original promise record are foundational and remain free. A paid plan could fund longer-term monitoring, searchable purchase history, warranty and return-date reminders, and exportable evidence bundles. Merchant or retailer integrations should improve the buyer's assurance record, never suppress negative comparisons.

Any referral relationship must be labeled at the product or seller offer and must not affect the deterministic condition comparison or ranking.

PayPal can benefit when a buyer chooses a familiar checkout after the Passport decision allows it: increased checkout use can support PayPal transaction volume, while the buyer gets a preserved record of the offer and a decision gate before the payment step. MIRROR does not promise a refund or replace PayPal's own dispute processes.
